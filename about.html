Our story

Started in a garage. Still run that way.

Maple & Mill began in 2018 as a single roaster in a rented garage. Six years later, the garage is a proper roastery around the corner — but the habit of doing things by hand hasn't changed.

How it started

Owner Priya Nair set up her first roaster in a rented garage after years of working other people's coffee shops. She wanted somewhere that felt more like a kitchen table than a chain — a place where regulars are greeted by name and the person pulling your espresso probably roasted it too.

The bakery came a year later, when her brother Sam joined with a background in pastry. Today the two of them run Maple & Mill together, with a small team of bakers, roasters, and baristas who've mostly stuck around since the early days.

What matters to us

Direct-trade beans

We buy directly from three family farms in Colombia and Ethiopia, and visit each one at least once a year.

Everything made from scratch

No frozen dough, no syrup pumps. If it's on the counter, someone in the kitchen made it that morning.

A neighborhood spot, first

We'd rather know your name than your order — though we're happy to remember both.

Who's behind the counter

Priya leads roasting and sourcing. Sam runs the bakery and manages the kitchen team. Our baristas are trained on-site over a full month before they pull espresso for a paying customer — we like to get it right.
