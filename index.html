Open daily on Birchwood Avenue

Coffee that's roasted three miles from where you're sitting.

Maple & Mill is a neighborhood coffee house and bakery. We roast in small batches, bake every morning before sunrise, and keep a table open for whoever walks in.

Reserve a table See the menu

What keeps people coming back

Small-batch roasting

We roast in-house twice a week, in batches small enough that the beans never sit on a shelf for long.

Baked before sunrise

Our bakers arrive at 4 a.m. so the croissants are ready before the first regulars walk in.

A place to stay a while

Big windows, mismatched chairs, and no pressure to give up your table after twenty minutes.

Find us on Birchwood Avenue

We're tucked between the hardware store and the old cinema, with a green door that's easy to miss the first time. Once you've found it, you won't forget it.
